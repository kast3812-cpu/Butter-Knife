Privacy Policy

WorkHours – Staff Attendance · Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the WorkHours – Staff Attendance Android app (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by Butter Knife Solutions, Inc (“we”, “us”). The App is designed to work on your device without sending your data to us.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information the App handles

WorkHours is an attendance tracker. The information below is entered by you (or the people who use the App in your organization) and is stored on the device:

DataPurpose
Employee names and departmentsTo identify who each attendance record belongs to
Attendance records: date, status (present, absent, on leave), check-in and check-out timesTo build timesheets, detect late arrivals and early departures, and calculate hours worked
Leave type (annual, sick, unpaid, other)To keep absence and leave records and leave balances
Optional notes on a recordTo add context to a record

The App does not ask for, and we do not collect, your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or device identifiers.

3. Where your data is stored

All attendance data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server for the App, and the App does not require an account or sign-in. We cannot see, access or recover your data.

Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in its automatic device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings, not by us.

4. We do not sell or share your data

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • The App does not transmit your attendance data over the internet.

5. Exporting reports

The App can create CSV reports. When you export, you choose what happens to the file:

  • Save CSV: you choose where the file is saved on your device or cloud storage using the Android file picker.
  • Share: you choose the app (for example email or a messaging app) that receives the report.

Once a report leaves the App, it is handled under the privacy practices of the app or service you selected. Please share reports only with people who are authorized to see them.

6. Permissions

The App does not request any special Android permissions. It does not need access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or general storage. Files are saved only to locations you select yourself.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • edit or delete individual records inside the App;
  • use Reports → Data → Clear all to remove every record; or
  • uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings, which removes the locally stored data.

Exported files and backups you created are not removed automatically; delete them separately if you no longer need them.

8. Employer and employee data

If you use WorkHours to record attendance of other people (for example, employees), you are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so, for informing those people, and for complying with the labor and data-protection laws that apply to you. We do not receive or process that data and are not a party to the relationship between you and the people whose attendance you record.

9. Security

Data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. Its security also depends on your device. We recommend using a screen lock, keeping Android up to date, and protecting exported reports.

10. Children

The App is intended for workplace use by adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, you control it directly in the App. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction or deletion. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date at the top will change. If the App begins collecting or sharing data in a new way, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
