Doneify Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Doneify is an offline work task manager. Your privacy matters, and this app is built so that your information never leaves your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, or store any of your personal information. Doneify has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers.

Data stored on your device

The tasks, checklists, notes, name, and settings you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent anywhere. You can delete all tasks at any time from Settings using "Reset data", or by uninstalling the app.

Internet and permissions

Doneify works completely offline. It does not use the internet and does not request any Android permissions, so it cannot access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Ads, analytics, and tracking

Doneify contains no advertisements, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no tracking of any kind.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not have any of your data.

Children's privacy

Because Doneify collects no information, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
